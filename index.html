03 · How I work

Four habits that show up in every project

01

Make progress visible

RAG status reports, burndown charts and shared trackers mean nobody has to ask where things stand.

02

Agree who owns what

A RACI and a clear sign-off step for every decision, so work does not stall waiting for an owner.

03

Log risks early

A RAID log with owners and dates, reviewed every week, with escalation to the steering group when something is blocked.

04

Make the change stick

Training by role, a parallel run and a 30-day check-in, so the new way of working lasts after go-live.
